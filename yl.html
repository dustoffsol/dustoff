Dustoff

Get back the SOL
your wallet is holding.

Every token you've held leaves behind an empty account that locks up about 0.002 SOL in rent. Dustoff finds them and closes them so the SOL returns to you.

Reclaimable SOL
—
Connect a wallet to scan for empty accounts.

Only empty accounts

Accounts holding any balance are never touched. Only token accounts with exactly 0 tokens are closed.

You approve everything

Your wallet shows each transaction before it's signed. The rent goes back to your own address.

Closed in batches

Up to 20 accounts are closed per transaction to keep network fees low.

Reclaimed SOL is sent to the connected wallet, minus a 1% service fee that is shown before you sign and included in each transaction. Review every transaction in your wallet before approving. Use at your own risk.
